About me

I'm an AI engineer currently at Mavenir in Dallas, where I build agentic workflows that automate statement-of-work, bill-of-materials and customer-questionnaire review for the 5G-RAN operations team, cutting review time by 95%. Underneath that sits a multi-stage RAG pipeline over 45,000+ internal documents, orchestrated with LangGraph and served through FastAPI, with evaluation pipelines in Ragas and DeepEval guiding every chunking, reranker and model decision.

Before that I spent three years at Accenture as a software engineer on the Calypso capital markets platform, scaling Spring Boot microservices to 10K+ requests a day, cutting release time from 60 to 10 minutes with CI/CD, and building a Solr-backed retrieval service over 10K+ PDFs. I also built a serverless public-comment analysis pipeline for the USDA at the ASU AI Cloud Innovation Centre.

I recently completed my Master's in Computer Science at Arizona State University with a 4.0 GPA, focusing on cloud computing, data processing at scale and statistical machine learning, and I hold the AWS Solutions Architect Associate certification. Outside of work I build things at hackathons, where I've won five times. I'm interested in roles where AI and cloud engineering meet real automation problems.
